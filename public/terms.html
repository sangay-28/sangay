SHERUBTSE COLLEGE

Terms & data use

These terms explain how information submitted through club and event forms is used.

Membership applications

Your name, email, course, year, and selected club are shared with the relevant club coordinators so they can review your application and contact you about joining.

Event registrations

Your registration details are used by event coordinators to manage participation, communicate event information, and plan event logistics.

Contact messages

Your contact details and message are used by the Sherubtse Clubs team to respond to your enquiry.

Use and retention

Submit accurate information and only information needed for your request. Records are available to authorized administrators for club operations. Do not submit passwords, bank details, or other sensitive credentials through these forms.

To ask about a submission or request help, contact the Sherubtse Clubs team.
